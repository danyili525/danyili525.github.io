About me

Public health researcher studying how policies, environments, and interventions shape youth health behaviors and outcomes.

I am a PhD Candidate in Preventive Medicine (Health Behavior Research) at the Keck School of Medicine, University of Southern California with a pre-doctoral fellow award by California Tobacco-Related Disease Research Program (TRDRP). My dissertation examines the impact of California local and statewide flavored tobacco sales restrictions on youth e-cigarettes use behavior using quasi-experimental designs and causal inference framework.

My training is multidisciplinary, spanning social and developmental psychology, epidemiology, biostatistics, and public policy. Before joining USC, I earned a Master of Public Health in Biostatistics and Epidemiology, a Master of Social Policy at Washington University in St. Louis, and a Master of Public Finance and Policy at Renmin University of China.

My research focuses on how public health policies and services, prevention interventions, built environments, and other systemic social determinants of health shape health behaviors and outcomes among youth, especially substance use, addictive behaviors, and obesity-related behaviors and outcomes.

Specifically, my work applies:

  • Quasi-experimental designs to study natural experiments such as tobacco-control policies and natural disasters;
  • Observational cross-sectional and longitudinal designs with regression-based and machine-learning methods on large population-level survey data;
  • Clustered randomized controlled trials to evaluate school-based prevention interventions;
  • Qualitative methods including thematic analysis on social media content and in-depth interviews with health providers in substance use treatment centers and schools.

Additional information about my work can be found in the publications and projects sections, which offer deeper insights into the research questions I investigate and the methods I employ. For inquiries, potential collaborations, or to share thoughts, feel free to get in touch by sending me a note.
